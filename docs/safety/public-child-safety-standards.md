# Betweener Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Betweener has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
These standards apply to every Betweener member and to all profiles, messages,
images, video, audio, documents, Circles, Moments, and Live experiences.

## Prohibited conduct

Members must not create, upload, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate:

- child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including generated or manipulated media;
- grooming, sextortion, sexualisation of a child, or sexual contact with a child;
- trafficking, coercion, or arranging the exploitation of a child;
- instructions, links, coded language, or off-platform redirection for CSAE; or
- threats, retaliation, or attempts to conceal or preserve prohibited material.

Suspected violations may result in immediate content removal, account
restriction or termination, preservation of relevant evidence, and reports to
the appropriate authority where required by law. Betweener does not provide
copies of suspected illegal material to members or unauthorised third parties.

## How members can report

Use the visible Report control on a profile, conversation, Moment, Circle item,
or Live safety surface. Members can also open the in-app Trust Center and choose
Contact the safety team. For urgent concerns, email hello@getbetweener.com
with the subject "Urgent child-safety concern". Do not attach or forward
suspected CSAM; identify the account or in-app content instead.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law
enforcement first.

## Our response

Betweener reviews child-safety reports as a priority, restricts access while
necessary, preserves auditable enforcement records, and takes action under
these standards and applicable law. Material confirmed through Betweener's
authorised review process is removed. Where reporting is legally required, an
authorised reviewer follows the applicable regional reporting process.
Betweener does not claim automated external reporting or connection to a
third-party known-CSAM hash feed.

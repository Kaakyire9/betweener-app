# Betweener Live — store review notes

## Product boundary

Betweener Live is an identified, consent-led community experience. It is not anonymous or unrestricted random chat.

- Public Live sessions are created by Betweener administrators and hosted by an administrator or a session-scoped delegated host.
- Delegated host authority is limited to one Live and expires when that Live ends.
- Quick Connect enrolment is explicit. Pairing is facilitated by the host or Odo and requires the product's eligibility and safety checks.
- Private Spark requires mutual consent. Camera and microphone remain under each participant's control.

## Safety controls available to reviewers

- The shield in the public Live header privately reports the room.
- Selecting a participant opens private `Report` and `Block` actions.
- Room Pulse comments expose comment reporting; hosts and moderators can remove comments.
- Hosts and moderators can mute, remove, or suspend a participant.
- Private Spark and Quick Connect include exit, report, and block controls.
- Reports enter the durable Live safety queue and are never announced in the room.

## Review path

1. Sign in with the review account supplied in App Store Connect or Play Console.
2. Open **Circles** and select the **Live now** card.
3. Enter the Live as audience. Camera and microphone remain off unless the reviewer accepts a stage invitation or explicitly joins an eligible experience.
4. Tap the shield in the header to inspect room reporting.
5. Tap a participant identity badge to inspect participant report and block controls.
6. Expand **Room Pulse** to inspect reactions, comments, comment reporting, and moderation behaviour.
7. For Quick Connect, explicitly join the pool and follow the consent prompts. A pairing moves to a private two-person space only after the guarded server transition.

Do not place account credentials, private session identifiers, or production secrets in this repository. Supply review credentials only through the stores' secure review fields.
